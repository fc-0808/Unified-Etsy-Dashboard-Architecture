4PX shipping is the freight you pay 4PX (settled in CNY), shown separately from Etsy's payout ledger. Each order is priced immediately from 4PX's live rate card (ds.xms.estimated_cost.get — the figure 4PX shows once a parcel is measured) and marked estimated; once 4PX financially settles the consignment the dashboard reconciles it to the billed amount via ds.xms.order.getFreight. Use the + 4PX shipping toggle above to deduct these costs (converted into each shop's payout currency at the current rate) from your Etsy net for a true landed profit. Click any 4PX order number below to open the full carrier timeline (customs scans, delays, etc.).

Earnings come straight from each shop's Etsy payment-account ledger, shown in that shop's payout currency (never converted between currencies). The headline Net profit (and Sales, Fees & deductions, Marketing) reflect the selected date range. The Net profit · [month] line beneath it is the running total for the current calendar month (1st → today) regardless of the range — it mirrors Etsy's monthly Activity summary and can differ from the headline whenever the range isn't exactly this month. Current balance is the live amount held in the Etsy payment account right now and is not affected by the date range. Offsite Ads / Etsy Ads are billed at the shop level (counted in Marketing) and appear in the breakdown, not per order.

API Budget
The 5,000/day limit explained

Etsy gives every API app 5,000 calls per 24 hours — per API key, not per shop. If you have 5 shops using the same key, they all share one 5,000-call bucket.

It is a sliding window, not a fixed midnight reset. If you use 1,000 calls at 9 AM, those 1,000 slots open back up at 9 AM the next day. The budget refills gradually throughout the day, not all at once.

What uses calls? Every time the dashboard fetches orders, checks tracking status, refreshes inventory, or sends a shipment update to Etsy — each one costs 1 call. Background syncs are the biggest consumer.

Why do I get blocked? When the budget runs out, the dashboard pauses background syncs automatically (shown as amber "Rate-limited" on the shop card). Critical actions like completing orders still go through using a reserved pool. The dashboard resumes on its own as the window rolls forward and budget is freed up — typically within a few minutes to an hour.

The history below shows every Etsy call we made, newest first. The Reason column tells you what each call was for (fetching orders, checking inventory, syncing messages, shipping an order, etc.). Remaining is how many calls Etsy said were left right after that call, and Calls used is the change since the previous reading. A green "+N (window released)" means the rolling 24-hour window freed up old calls — your budget actually went up.
